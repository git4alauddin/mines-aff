MINES / GAME GUIDE

ONE TILE AT A TIME

How to play

  1. Set your field. Choose 1–20 mines and a virtual bet.
  2. Find the stars. Press BET, then open a tile. Each star increases your multiplier.
  3. Make your move. Cash out after any star, or keep opening tiles. A mine ends the round.

RANDOM opens an unrevealed tile. For Auto Game, switch it on, select tiles, set the number of rounds, then press BET.

This is a local creator simulation. Layouts are predetermined. The preview panel shows local presets and cannot predict an external game.
